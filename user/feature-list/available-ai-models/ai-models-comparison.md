# AI Models Comparison

This guide helps you quickly compare available AI models on Doraverse, so you can choose the best one for your tasks and needs.&#x20;

## What We Compare

We use these five key metrics to compare the AI models:

* **Intelligence (Massive Multitask Language Understanding)**\
  This is a score that shows how “smart” the model is, based on tests with lots of different questions. The higher the number, the better the model is at understanding and answering questions.
* **Speed**\
  How quickly the model can process information and generate predictions or outputs. Faster speed means you get answers more quickly.
* **Latency**\
  The time delay between when an AI system receives an input and generates the corresponding output. In other words, it's how long you have to wait for the model to start giving you an answer. Lower numbers mean less waiting.&#x20;
* **Price**\
  How much it costs to use the model for 1 million tokens. Lower prices are cheaper to use.
