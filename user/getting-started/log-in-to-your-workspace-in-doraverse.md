# Log in to Your Workspace in Doraverse

As a team member, there are two simple ways to access your workspace in Doraverse for log in:

## 1. Access workspace through the invitation email

* Check your business email inbox for an invitation from your workspace admin.
* Open the email — you’ll find a direct link to your workspace and the email address to use.

💡**Tip:** If you don’t see the email, check your spam/promotions folder or reach out to your administrator.

## 2. Access directly to your workspace URL

* If you already know your team or business workspace URL in Doraverse, simply enter it in your browser.\
  It will look like: `https://yourcompany.doraverse.com`\
  &#xNAN;_&#x45;xample:_ `https://case.doraverse.com`

#### What’s Next?

1. Enter your business email address on the login page
2. Check your inbox to get the verification code
3. Enter the code and click **Continue**.

That’s it — welcome to your workspace! You’re ready to chat, collaborate, and unlock the power of Doraverse with your team.
