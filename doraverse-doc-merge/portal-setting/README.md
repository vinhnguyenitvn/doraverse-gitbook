## What is Portal Setting?

Portal Settings allow administrators to customize and manage core configurations of your  AI workspace in Doraverse. These settings ensure your portal aligns with your company’s operational needs, branding and AI model preferences.

## What’s Included in Portal Setting?

### Brand Setting

* Upload your company logo, brand name, and tagline.
* Adjust UI themes to match your corporate identity.

### Setup AI Models

Enable/disable specific models for different departments to meet their needs.

### Setup Limits

Define usage quotas for  workspace or individuals (e.g., API calls, chat interactions).
