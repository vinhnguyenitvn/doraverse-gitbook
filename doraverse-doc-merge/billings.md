# 🧾 Billings

Our billing process is designed for flexibility, ensuring charges reflect both the subscription and the workspace’s actual usage:

* When the admin subscribes, a base subscription fee is charged, which includes up to 5 seats and $50 in monthly credits.
* If the admin invites more than 5 members, additional seat fees ($9/seat/mo for the monthly subscription) will apply. The number of active seats is recalculated at the start of each new billing cycle.
* The admin can add or remove seats at any time during the billing period. All seat changes are automatically prorated and reflected on the next invoice, whether it’s a monthly or annual plan.
* If the team uses up both the included monthly credits and free credits, the admin can purchase prepaid credits to ensure uninterrupted access.

***

## Charges <a href="#charges" id="charges"></a>

All billing is handled through Stripe. Doraverse doesn’t store any sensitive payment information, and you can manage all your billing information through the **Billing** tab in your Admin Workspace.

### **When and how you’re charged:**

* On subscription activation (trial → subscription) or upgrade (monthly → annual).
* At the start of each billing cycle, based on your current total number of seats.

***

## **Manage Billing & Invoice**

Go to **Account → Admin → Billing → Overview → Manage Billing** to:

* View your billing and credit usage history&#x20;
* Update payment methods&#x20;
* View and download invoices
