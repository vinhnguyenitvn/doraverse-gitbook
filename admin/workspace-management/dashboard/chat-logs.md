# Chat Logs

[What’s included in Chat Log?](chat-logs.md#whats-included-in-chat-log)

## **What are Chat Logs?**

Chat Logs give you a complete overview of all conversations with AI models and agents within your workspace. Use Chat Logs to:

* Track user interactions for greater accountability and transparency.
* Analyze chat content to improve quality, ensure compliance, and optimize processes.
* Retrieve past conversations for easy reference and collaboration.
* Monitor chat activity across departments to ensure data security and oversight.

This centralized view helps admins and managers maintain control, streamline workflows, and support team productivity with full visibility into AI-powered communications.

## **What’s included in Chat Log?**

* **Title**: Conversation title for quick recognition.
* **User**: The member who initiated or participated in the chat.
* **Model**: The AI model that handled the conversation.
* **Time**: Exact date and time the chat took place.
* **Agent**: The specific agent used during the session.
